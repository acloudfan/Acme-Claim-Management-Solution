# Customer FAQ Knowledge Base

## Claims Process

### How long does it take to process a claim?
Most claims are processed within 24-48 hours. If your claim requires human review, it may take 3-5 business days.

**Keywords:** processing time, how long, duration, wait time

### What information do I need to submit a claim?
You'll need photos of the damage, your policy number, vehicle information (make, model, year), and details about when and where the damage occurred.

**Keywords:** submit claim, requirements, what do I need, information needed

### Can I track my claim status?
Yes! You can check your claim status anytime through your customer portal or by asking our chatbot.

**Keywords:** track claim, claim status, check status, where is my claim

### What happens after I submit photos?
Our AI system analyzes your photos to detect damage and estimate repair costs. You'll receive a decision within 24-48 hours.

**Keywords:** after submission, next steps, what happens next

### Can I add more photos after submitting?
Yes! If you need to add more photos, you can upload them from your claim detail page. This may restart the review process.

## Cost Estimates

### Why is my estimate so high?
Repair costs depend on the extent of damage, parts needed, and labor rates in your area. Our estimates are based on fair market rates for your region.

**Keywords:** high cost, expensive, estimate too high, why so much

### How are repair costs calculated?
We calculate costs based on the type and severity of damage, market rates for parts, and average labor rates in your state. Each damage type has a baseline cost that's adjusted for severity.

**Keywords:** calculate cost, how costs work, pricing, how you calculate

### Does my estimate include tax?
Yes, all estimates include applicable state and local taxes, as well as any environmental fees required in your area.

**Keywords:** tax, fees, additional charges, total cost

### Why is labor so expensive?
Labor rates vary by location and reflect the skill level required for proper repairs. Our rates are based on average market rates for licensed technicians in your area.

**Keywords:** labor cost, labor rate, why labor expensive

### Can I get a second opinion on the estimate?
Yes! You can appeal your estimate, and a human adjustor will review it within 2-3 business days. You can also get independent estimates from repair shops.

**Keywords:** second opinion, disagree with estimate, other estimates

## Appeals

### What happens if I appeal my estimate?
If you appeal, a human adjustor will review your claim within 2-3 business days. They may adjust the estimate or request additional photos.

**Keywords:** appeal, dispute, disagree, not satisfied

### How do I submit an appeal?
Click the "Appeal Decision" button on your claim detail page and provide your reason for the appeal. You can also upload additional photos or documentation.

**Keywords:** submit appeal, appeal process, how to appeal, file appeal

### What should I include in my appeal?
Explain why you believe the estimate is incorrect, and provide any supporting evidence such as quotes from repair shops, additional photos, or documentation of the damage.

**Keywords:** appeal details, what to include, appeal documentation

### How long does an appeal take?
Most appeals are reviewed within 2-3 business days. Complex cases may take up to 5 business days.

**Keywords:** appeal time, how long appeal, appeal duration

## Policy Coverage

### What does my policy cover?
Coverage depends on your policy type. Collision coverage handles damage from accidents, while comprehensive covers non-collision incidents like hail or vandalism.

**Keywords:** coverage, what's covered, policy coverage, insurance coverage

### What is my deductible?
Your deductible is the amount you pay before insurance coverage kicks in. You can find your deductible amount on your policy documents or by asking our chatbot.

**Keywords:** deductible, out of pocket, what I pay, how much I pay

### Does my policy cover rental cars?
Rental car coverage depends on your policy. Check your policy documents or contact us to confirm if you have rental reimbursement coverage.

**Keywords:** rental car, rental coverage, car rental

### What's the difference between collision and comprehensive coverage?
Collision covers damage from accidents with other vehicles or objects. Comprehensive covers non-collision events like theft, vandalism, hail, or flooding.

**Keywords:** collision vs comprehensive, difference, types of coverage

### Am I covered if someone else was driving my car?
In most cases, your insurance follows the vehicle, not the driver. However, coverage may vary based on your policy and who was driving. Contact us for specifics.

**Keywords:** other driver, someone else driving, friend driving

## Repair Shops

### Can I choose my own repair shop?
Yes! While we recommend shops in our network for quality and convenience, you're free to choose any licensed repair facility.

**Keywords:** choose shop, repair shop, my mechanic, own shop

### What are the benefits of using a network shop?
Network shops offer guaranteed work quality, streamlined claim processing, and often faster appointment availability.

**Keywords:** network shop, benefits, preferred provider, why network shop

### How do I find recommended repair shops?
Click "Find Repair Shops" on your claim detail page to see a list of recommended shops near you, complete with ratings, specialties, and availability.

**Keywords:** find shops, recommended shops, repair shop list

### Do I have to use a recommended shop?
No, you're not required to use our recommended shops. You can choose any licensed repair facility. However, we can only guarantee work done at network shops.

**Keywords:** required shop, have to use, must use network shop

### What if the shop finds additional damage?
If additional damage is discovered during repairs, the shop should contact us immediately. We'll review the new damage and adjust your claim if approved.

**Keywords:** more damage, additional damage, hidden damage, extra repairs
